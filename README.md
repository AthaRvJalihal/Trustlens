🛡️ TRUSTLENS — Cyber Threat & Scam AnalyzerZero-Telemetry, Client-Side Heuristic Cyber Threat & Social Engineering Detector (v2.4)📌 OverviewTRUSTLENS is an open-source, client-side threat intelligence and scam analysis workbench. Designed in response to surging real-world attack vectors—including "Digital Arrest" video extortion, AI voice-cloning emergencies, KYC/PAN smishing, and UPI collect fraud—TRUSTLENS parses unstructured messages and extracts actionable threat indicators without sending user data to any remote server.✨ Key Features🔒 100% Client-Side Inspection: Zero outbound telemetry. Evaluated entirely within the browser via JavaScript regular expressions and deterministic heuristic weighting.⚡ 11-Rule Deterministic Engine: Matches critical vectors (P0/P1) across banking, urgency manipulation, malware/APK links, and law enforcement impersonation.🎯 Visual Threat Meter & Sub-Scores: Radial SVG progress gauge displaying unified risk ($0 - 100$) and decomposed sub-scores for Urgency & Manipulation vs. Credential & Financial Risk.🔍 Content Highlighter: Color-codes dangerous (hl-danger) and suspicious (hl-warn) keywords directly within the analyzed content preview.📊 Incident Telemetry Dashboard: Overview of local inspection volumes, P0/P1 threat distribution, and regional telemetry presets.📚 Threat Intelligence Library: Filterable database of active social engineering tactics with one-click preset test scenarios.💾 Local Audit History: Persists past scans in the browser's localStorage with re-analysis support and instant incident verification checklists.⚙️ Heuristic Scoring EngineThe engine computes an aggregated risk score normalized from $0$ to $100$.Severity ThresholdsScore RangeVerdictAction Required56 – 100CRITICAL / DANGEROUS (P0)Severe threat indicators present.
