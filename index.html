About TRUSTLENS & Cyber Safety Guide

Rule #01 · Critical Law Enforcement Fact

"Digital Arrest" Does Not Exist in Law

Police, CBI, Customs, Reserve Bank, or Cybercrime officers will never interrogate you over WhatsApp/Skype video calls or ask you to transfer money to a "Reserve Bank verification account." Hang up immediately.

Rule #02 · Credential & UPI Hygiene

OTPs and UPI PINs Are Strictly for Outgoing Money

You never need to enter a UPI PIN, scan a QR code, or read out an SMS OTP to receive a refund, cashback, or lottery payout. Anyone asking for a code to send you money is authorizing a withdrawal from your account.

Rule #03 · Psychological Defense

Defeat Artificial Urgency with a 2-Minute Pause

Threats of "electricity cut tonight at 9:30 PM", "SIM card deactivation in 2 hours", or "son arrested in accident" are designed to trigger adrenaline and bypass critical thinking. Pause, disconnect, and call the official number independently.

Rule #04 · Device Integrity

Never Install APK Files or Screen-Sharing Apps for "Support"

Fraudsters send Bank-KYC-Update.apk on WhatsApp or ask you to install AnyDesk/RustDesk to help unblock a card. These grant full remote view of your banking SMS OTPs.

🚨 Emergency Incident Response

Already Clicked or Shared an OTP?

  1. Immediately turn on Airplane Mode if you installed an unknown app or screen-share tool.
  2. Call the National Cybercrime Helpline at 1930 within the "Golden Hour" to freeze the fraudulent recipient bank transfer.
  3. File a formal complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and lock your UPI/NetBanking via your bank's toll-free number.

Cross-Check & Verification Protocol

Follow this 3-step manual verification checklist before taking any action on a suspicious message:

1. Break the Communication Channel Hang up the call or close the SMS/WhatsApp thread. Never reply to or call back a phone number embedded inside a suspicious text.
2. Independent Official Lookup Open your official banking app directly or dial the customer care number printed on the back of your physical debit/credit card.
3. Check Root Domain & Headers Regulated Indian bank messages carry authorized TRAI headers (e.g., AX-HDFCBK-S), never personal 10-digit mobile numbers (+91 98xxxxxxxx).
